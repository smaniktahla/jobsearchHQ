Add Job

Put a job you found into JSHQ yourself.

Do this first

  1. Pick a tab: Paste JD (paste the job description text), From URL (a link to the posting) or Email Forward (paste an email about a job).
  2. Click Add & Auto-Extract. JSHQ reads the text and fills in the title, company and pay for you.
  3. Open the job in the Tracker to score it.

Common questions

Which tab works best?

Pasting the full job description is the most reliable. A link only works when the website lets JSHQ read the page.

My link didn't work. What now?

Some sites block automatic reading. Open the posting yourself, copy the description, and use the Paste JD tab instead.

Will it be scored automatically?

Only if you turned on automatic scoring in Job Search & Automation. Otherwise open the job and click Score This Job.
