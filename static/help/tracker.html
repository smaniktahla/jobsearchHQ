Tracker

Every job you have added or found, in one list you can filter and sort.

Do this first

  1. Use the filters at the top to narrow the list: resume lane, whether the posting is still open, documents, and status.
  2. Click a column heading to sort. Sorting by SCORE shows the best fits first.
  3. Click a job to open it.

Common questions

What does the score mean?

It is a rating from 0 to 10 of how well the job fits your background, pay targets and interests. As a rule of thumb: 8 to 10 is a strong fit, 5 to 7 is worth a look, under 5 is probably not for you.

What is a Resume Lane?

You may have more than one version of your resume. JSHQ picks the one that suits each job best and shows it in the RESUME LANE column. You can change it on the job's page. Names come from Resumes.

What does the pencil next to a score mean?

You entered your own score for that job. The AI's score is kept in the job's score history.

What does Ready mean?

The job is approved, has a tailored resume and cover letters, and you haven't applied yet. It is ready for you to send.

What does Score All Unscored do?

It rates every job that has no score yet, using your AI provider. It can take a while and may use paid credits.

Good to know

  • Jobs that are old and scored low move to the Archive automatically. They are not deleted.
