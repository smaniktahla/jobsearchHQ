Search Boards

Look for jobs on several job websites at once, plus the career pages of companies you name.

Do this first

  1. Type a job title or keywords in Search Term and a place in Location (for example Washington, DC or Remote).
  2. Tick the Job Boards you want, choose how many results and how recent, then click Search.
  3. Turn on Auto-Score if you want each result rated for fit right away, then review the results.

Common questions

Why do I see jobs that don't fit me?

Job sites match loosely: a word from your search can appear anywhere in a posting. Turn on Auto-Score (or use Score All With Descriptions) and look at the highest scores first.

Why did a board return nothing?

Job sites sometimes block or change. The results note says which board had trouble. Try the other boards; Google in particular often returns nothing.

How long does a search take?

From a few seconds to a few minutes. Getting full descriptions is the slow part. You can press Stop search at any time.

What does Fetch full descriptions do?

It opens each posting to read the whole description. It is slower but gives the scoring much better information, so it is recommended.

Does scoring cost money?

It uses the AI provider you chose in AI settings. Some providers have a free tier; others charge per use.

Good to know

  • Remote only, Hybrid and In-person narrow the results to the kind of work you want.
  • To search for the same titles every day automatically, see Job Search & Automation.
