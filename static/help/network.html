Network

Your private contact list, built from your LinkedIn connections. This page only appears if it has been turned on.

Do this first

  1. Download your connections file from LinkedIn and choose it under Import LinkedIn connections (up to 10 MB).
  2. Click Preview import to see what would change, then Commit import to save it.
  3. Use Search and the Employer filter to find people at a company.

Common questions

Why does it say “unverified”?

Imported information may be out of date or wrong. Contacts stay unverified until you confirm them.

How do I remove everything?

Use Delete all network data. You can also use Export first to keep a copy.
