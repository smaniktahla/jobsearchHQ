Profile

Your details. They are used to fill in resumes and cover letters, and to judge how well jobs fit you.

Do this first

  1. Fill in Personal Information: name, email, phone and links such as LinkedIn. The links appear in the contact line of every resume.
  2. Add your Work Experience, Education, Certifications and Languages.
  3. Set your Pay Targets, then list any Deal Breakers. Click Save Profile at the bottom.

Common questions

What are Pay Targets?

The yearly salary range you want for a regular job, and the hourly rate you want for contract work. Jobs are scored partly on how well the pay matches.

What are Deal Breakers?

Things you will not accept, one per line, for example “must relocate”. Jobs that mention them are flagged.
