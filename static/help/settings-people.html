People Search

Add services that find hiring managers and recruiters when you click Research Company on a job.

Do this first

  1. Sign up with one of the services and copy its API key. People Data Labs and Hunter both have free plans.
  2. Tick the service, paste the key into its box and click Test Key. It saves first, then checks the key. Testing uses no credits.
  3. Open a job and click Research Company. People found this way are labelled with the service's name.

Common questions

Which service should I pick?

People Data Labs looks for people by job title, so it is best for finding the likely hiring manager. Hunter finds work email addresses at the company. Apollo does both, but only on a paid Apollo plan.

What kind of people does it look for?

It starts from each job's own title. A nursing job looks for nursing leaders; an engineering job looks for engineering leaders. Under Who to look for you can add titles that matter for every job, and choose whether to include recruiters.

Will this use up my free credits?

Only when you ask. By default only the Research Company button uses these services. Results for a company are reused for 30 days, so researching a second job there costs nothing. You can also choose to stop at the first service that finds people.

Why does an Apollo name look like “Sam Sm***h”?

Apollo hides part of the name until you click Reveal on that person. Each Reveal uses one Apollo credit.

Apollo says my plan has no access.

Apollo's free plan does not let other apps use its people search. Use People Data Labs or Hunter instead, or upgrade Apollo.

Can other people on this server use my key?

No. Keys and credits belong to your account only. Other accounts need their own keys.

Good to know

  • If web research is not working, Research Company still returns what these services find.
  • Automatic research leaves these services alone unless you tick the box under Credits.
