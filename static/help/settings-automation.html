Job Search & Automation

Decide what JSHQ does by itself: a daily search, automatic scoring, and keeping the Tracker tidy.

Do this first

  1. To search every day, tick Enable daily automated search, choose the time, and list your Job titles to search (one per line).
  2. Choose the job boards, location and how recent results should be.
  3. Decide whether to Automatically score new jobs. This ranks results by fit but uses your AI provider. Then click save.

Common questions

Why do I get jobs that don't fit?

Each title is searched separately and job sites match loosely. Automatic scoring ranks the results so the best fits rise to the top.

What does the pause switch do?

Job search running can be turned off to stop new jobs being added, read or scored. Your existing jobs are not touched.

What is “Keep tracking jobs scoring at least”?

Jobs that score lower than this are moved to the Archive after a few days so the Tracker stays quick. They are not deleted.

What does the nightly check do?

Every night JSHQ checks whether the jobs you are tracking are still open and marks the closed ones.

What does Run Pipeline Now do?

It runs the daily routine immediately instead of waiting for the scheduled time.
