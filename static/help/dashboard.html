Dashboard

Your home page. It shows where your job search stands at a glance.

Do this first

  1. Click + Add Job to add a job you found, or use Search Boards to find jobs for you.
  2. Open a job from Recent Jobs to see how well it fits you.
  3. If this is your first visit, make sure your Profile, a resume and an AI provider are set up. The scores depend on them.

Common questions

What are the numbers at the top?

They count your jobs by stage: new, scored, applied, interview and offer. A job moves along as you work on it.

Why is it empty?

You haven't added or searched for any jobs yet. Add one, or run a search.

What does the pause message mean?

Someone paused your job search, so nothing new is being added, read or scored. You can start it again under Job Search & Automation.

What is the follow-up reminder?

After you mark a job as applied, JSHQ reminds you to follow up after a set number of days. View opens the list on the Follow-Ups page.
