Resumes

Your resume versions, and the layout and writing style used for the ones JSHQ prepares for you.

Do this first

  1. Under Resume Names, give each version a name you will recognize.
  2. Under Upload Resume Text, choose the version and upload a Word file or paste the text. Also add your Full Employment History, which is what scoring reads.
  3. Under Resume Lanes, say in a sentence what kind of job each resume is for, then click Save Lane Descriptions.

Common questions

What is a Resume Lane?

Each resume is a lane. JSHQ puts every job in the lane that fits best and uses that resume as the starting point.

What is the Resume Template for?

Upload a Word file whose look you like. Tailored resumes copy its fonts, headings and spacing. Simple layouts work best: photos, text boxes and side columns do not copy over.

What are Writing Samples?

Examples of your own writing. When JSHQ writes cover letters and resumes, it matches your tone. You can turn this off with the checkbox.

Why does my tailored resume look different from my original?

JSHQ rebuilds the resume from text, so decorative layouts are replaced by a clean standard one. Check the result before you send it.
