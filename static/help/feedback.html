Sending feedback

Tell us when something is confusing, broken or could be better. It goes straight to the people who run JSHQ.

Do this first

  1. Open Help and pick a button at the bottom: I need more help, Something isn't right or I have an idea.
  2. Describe it in your own words. The more detail the better.
  3. Click Send. You will see a reference number.

Common questions

What gets sent?

Your message, the name of the page you were on, your browser and screen size, and a short list of recent error messages if there were any. Never your password or your resume. A screenshot is only included if you add one.

How do I add a screenshot?

In the feedback form, click Capture this page to take a picture of what you see, or paste an image with Ctrl+V, or choose a file. Then drag over anything private, such as names or email addresses, to cover it with a black box. What you see in the preview is exactly what is sent. Tick the box to say you are happy to include it, then click Send.

Is my screenshot private?

Only the people who run JSHQ can see it. It is kept so the problem can be fixed, and it is never shown to other users. Cover anything you would rather not share before you send it.

Will I get a reply?

Every message is read. We reply when there is something useful to tell you, for example that a fix is on the way, so it can take a little while. Click See what I've sent at the bottom of Help to check where things stand.

Who can see my feedback?

Only the people who run JSHQ. It is not shown to other users.

I don't see the buttons.

Feedback has to be switched on for your JSHQ. If the buttons are missing, ask the person who runs it.

My message didn't send.

Your text is kept in the box. Wait a few minutes and press Send again.
