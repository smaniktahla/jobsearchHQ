Email & Follow-Up

Lets JSHQ send you emails and send application emails, and sets how soon to follow up.

Do this first

  1. Set Follow-Up After (days) and the address to be notified at.
  2. For Gmail, enter your Gmail address and a Gmail App Password: a special 16-character password you create in your Google account (it needs 2-Step Verification turned on; search “Google app passwords”). Your normal password will not work.
  3. Leave the email server and port as they are for Gmail, click Send Test Email, and check your inbox.

Common questions

Why an App Password?

Google does not let other programs use your normal password. An App Password is a separate one that only works for this purpose and can be removed any time.

What is the HTML digest option?

It makes the summary emails look nicer. Untick it if you prefer plain text.

When is the daily follow-up email sent?

The time is not set from this page yet; it is arranged by the person who runs your JSHQ. A simpler way to choose it is planned.

The test email didn't arrive.

Check your spam folder, then check the address and App Password. Look for a message just under the button; it usually says what went wrong.
