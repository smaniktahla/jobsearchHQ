Follow-Ups

Reminders to nudge employers after you apply.

Do this first

  1. Mark a job as applied (on its page). JSHQ then sets a follow-up date, by default 14 days later. You can change the number of days under Email & Follow-Up.
  2. Check Upcoming for what is due.
  3. Click Send Email Digest to email yourself the list.

Common questions

Why didn't the email arrive?

Email sending has to be set up first, and you can check it with Send Test Email under Email & Follow-Up.

How do I clear a reminder?

Open the job and click I Followed Up or Snooze 7d.
