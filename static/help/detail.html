Job details

Everything about one job: how well it fits you, the documents, contacts and follow-ups.

Do this first

  1. Read the Score Breakdown to see why the job scored the way it did.
  2. If you disagree, type Your score (0 to 10) and click Save my score. The AI's score stays on record under Score history, and your score is the one used everywhere.
  3. When it looks worth pursuing, create a Tailored Resume (.docx) and Cover Letters, then use Mark Applied after you send them.

Common questions

What are Flags & Gaps?

Red flags are warning signs in the posting, such as something on your deal-breaker list. Gaps are skills the job asks for that your resume doesn't show.

What is Gut Interest?

Your own feeling about the job: 0 (meh) or 1 (interested). It adds a point to the score.

What is the resume lane dropdown?

It shows which of your resumes JSHQ chose for this job (“auto”). Pick a different one if you know better. Names come from Resumes.

How do I find people at the company?

Click Research Company. It looks for key contacts and saves them on the job.

What does Check if still open do?

It looks at the posting to see whether the job is still available, and marks closed ones.

What do I Followed Up and Snooze 7d do?

They update the reminder: the first records that you followed up; the second moves the reminder out by a week.
