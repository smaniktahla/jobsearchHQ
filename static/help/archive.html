Archive

Older jobs that you no longer need in the Tracker. They stay on file.

Do this first

  1. Look here if a job seems to have disappeared from the Tracker.
  2. Click Run Archive Now to tidy up straight away instead of waiting for the nightly clean-up.

Common questions

What gets archived?

Jobs that are old and either rejected or passed on, or that scored below the minimum you set under Job Search & Automation (“Keep tracking jobs scoring at least”).

Is anything deleted?

No. Archived jobs stay on file; they just stop cluttering the Tracker.
