Setup wizard

A short walk-through that gets JSHQ ready to find and rate jobs for you.

Do this first

  1. About you: your name, contact details and background.
  2. Your resume: upload or paste it so jobs can be compared with it.
  3. AI provider: choose the service and paste its key, then press Save & test.
  4. What you want: the job titles, place, work arrangement and pay you are looking for.

Common questions

Can I stop and come back?

Yes. Each step is saved when you press Next, so you can pick up where you left off.

How do I run it again?

Open Account and click Run setup again. Nothing is erased; every step is filled in with your earlier answers.
