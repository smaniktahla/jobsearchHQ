Choose the AI service that scores jobs and writes your documents.

Do this first

  1. Read the short guide at the top of the page, then choose a provider for Fast and for Strong.
  2. Paste your key into the field for that provider and click Test Fast Provider or Test Strong Provider. It saves first, then checks the key works. Please allow up to two minutes.
  3. Optional: pick a specific model. Leave it on automatic if you are unsure.

Common questions

What are Fast and Strong?

Fast does the high-volume work, such as scoring many jobs, so choose something cheap or free. Strong writes your resumes and cover letters, so choose the best writer you are willing to pay for. They can be different.

Is a ChatGPT, Claude or Google AI subscription enough?

No. Chat subscriptions do not include the key JSHQ needs. You need an API key from the provider's developer site, billed separately. Gemini is the exception: it has a free tier.

What does “429” or “quota” mean?

The account is out of credit or hit a free-tier limit. Add credit, or wait a minute and try again.

What does “invalid key” mean?

The key was copied wrongly or belongs to a different provider. Copy it again with no spaces.

The test is slow. Is something wrong?

Not necessarily. The newest models are sometimes busy. JSHQ tries other models by itself, which can take a little while.

What is a model, and what is automatic?

A model is the specific AI version. Automatic picks a good current one for your key. Choose one yourself if you want to control cost or speed.

Good to know

  • Your key is stored for your account only. Leaving a key blank uses the server's shared key, if there is one.
