Activity

A look at what JSHQ is doing right now and what it did recently.

Do this first

  1. Check In Progress to see work that is running, such as scoring a batch of jobs.
  2. Read Recent Activity for what finished.
  3. Click View Full Run History to see earlier automatic runs.

Common questions

Something looks stuck. What do I do?

Look here first. Large batches take a while. If nothing changes for a long time, press Refresh, and if it still looks wrong, tell the person who runs your JSHQ.

What are Batch Scoring and Scheduler?

Batch Scoring is when many jobs are rated at once. The Scheduler is your daily automatic search, if you turned it on.
